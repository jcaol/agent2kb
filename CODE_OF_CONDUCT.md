# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part in the project's issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Welcome new contributors, accept feedback professionally,
  and focus on what is best for the community.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private
  information and sexualised language or attention are not acceptable.
- Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may temporarily or
  permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks these rules.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately, using GitHub's "Report content" on the offending
comment, by opening a private security advisory on the repository (Security tab) and marking
it as a conduct issue, or by contacting the maintainers listed in the repository. All
reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.
