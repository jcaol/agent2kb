## 6. How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA bot asks you to sign by posting this exact comment on the pull


your future contributions to agent2kb under this version of the agreement. If you contribute
on behalf of your employer, make sure you are authorised to do so (section 4). You only sign
once; post `recheck` if the bot does not notice your signature.
